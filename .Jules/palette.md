## 2024-03-24 - Empty States Provide Guidance
**Learning:** Adding empty states to lists (like a lobby list) when no data is available provides essential context to the user. Instead of a blank area that looks broken or empty, it guides the user on what to do next (e.g. "Create one to get started!").
**Action:** Always consider what a dynamic list looks like when it's empty, and provide helpful copy or a call-to-action instead of leaving it blank.
